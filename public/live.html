An agent streams its progress to this board with worklog.mjs: start a session with its tasks, mark each step as it begins and ends, then finish. The board polls every 1.5 s, ticks tasks off as they are done, shows the story so far, and keeps each finished session here for an hour with a link to its itemised story.

node worklog.mjs step 1 --current "checking the localStorage scoping"
node worklog.mjs done 1
node worklog.mjs finish

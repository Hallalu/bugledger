Paste something above and every hidden character will be listed here — grouped, named, counted, with the plain-English risk of each.

How honest is this? This tool cleans third-party hidden characters, prompt-injection / steganography payloads and formatting artefacts. It is not an “AI-watermark remover” — the only deployed LLM text watermark (Google SynthID-Text) lives in word choice, not characters, and cannot be removed by stripping. Em-dashes, curly quotes and ellipses are valid human typography and are not proof of AI, so they’re left untouched unless you opt in.

Same engine powers the Bug Ledger scanner & API hardening, so no project silently stores hidden characters. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no tracking.

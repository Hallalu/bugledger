What it can and can't see
  • Reads: C2PA manifests in JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4/MOV/HEIC (who signed, what tool, digitalSourceType), IPTC DigitalSourceType, A1111 prompts, ComfyUI graphs, EXIF/XMP tool names, GPS, encoder tags.
  • Hidden text: zero-width, tag and bidi characters. The C2PA 2.3 text manifest is reported as provenance, not junk.
  • Pixelbake watermark: read straight from the pixels; survives re-saving and resizing, not cropping.
  • Can't see other invisible watermarks (Google SynthID, Meta Video Seal, Adobe TrustMark): those need their maker's detector. A clean result never proves a file wasn't made with AI.
  • Most platforms strip metadata on upload, so a downloaded social-media copy usually shows nothing.
  • For a whole project or live site: node ~/BugLedger/aiscan.mjs . or --url https://…, or the /aiscan sweep.

Privacy

What Breadledger keeps about you, why, and how to get it or have it removed. Last updated 31 October 2026.

What we keep

  • Your email and the name you choose: to sign you in (we email you a one-time code) and to write to you about your account.
  • The devices you're signed in on: browser, system, rough city, and when you signed in and were last seen, so you can see them and sign them out.
  • What your connected agent sends to your board: what you asked it, short descriptions of what it is doing, its plan and steps, and the screenshots you add. Only you can see your board.
  • Security records: sign-ins, sign-outs and code requests, with a one-way scrambled form of your IP address, to stop abuse.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or pass it to anyone for advertising.
  • We don't read your private board. It is shown only to the account it belongs to.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies. The one cookie we set keeps you signed in.

Your choices

  • Download your data any time from Your account.
  • Delete your account from Your account: you're signed out everywhere, every agent key is switched off, your email is removed and your boards stop showing to anyone. Board history stays stored, because the ledger is append-only, but it is shown to no one.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 15 minutes. You stay signed in for up to 90 days without using the site; sign out on any device from Your account.

Where it's kept

On the servers of the cloud provider that hosts Breadledger, protected in transit with HTTPS.
